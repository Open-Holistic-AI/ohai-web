## Section 2 — The problem

### Doctors spend more time typing than treating.

In a busy government or private outpatient ward, the record is the bottleneck. Notes get written after hours, or not at all. Patients travel twice — once to consult, again to collect a report. Coding, formulary checks and follow-ups fall through the cracks.

OHM removes the paperwork from the consultation and puts the hospital in the patient's pocket — without asking a single clinician to change how they work.

## Section 3 — Ambient documentation (the hero feature)

**Eyebrow:** AMBIENT LISTENING

### The doctor speaks. OHM writes the note.

A doctor records the consultation naturally — in Tamil, English or code-mixed speech. OHM transcribes it, understands it clinically, and produces a complete, structured medical record before the patient leaves the room.

- **Hands-free records** — no typing during the consultation.
- **Accurate in real ward conditions** — background noise, cross-talk, multiple speakers.
- **20+ Indian languages** — Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Kannada, Marathi, Bengali, Gujarati, Punjabi, English and more, all returned in clean clinical English.
- **Structured, not just a transcript** — diagnoses, medications, allergies, investigations, vitals and referrals, each in its own coded field.
- **The doctor always decides** — every field is editable; AI fills gaps, the clinician's edits always win.

**Proven result:** 90 minutes saved per doctor, every day, in a live government hospital deployment.

## Section 5 — Clinical intelligence at the point of care

**Eyebrow:** DECISION-SUPPORT INSIGHTS

### Advisory intelligence, always clinician-facing.

OHM surfaces patient-specific, guideline-aligned insights alongside the note — never inside it, never directive. The doctor stays the decider.

- **Red-flag and risk detection** — escalation prompts, safety-netting, critical alerts.
- **Guideline-gap and screening prompts** tuned to Indian national programmes (NTEP TB, immunisation, diabetes/hypertension care, antenatal, and more).
- **Specialty-aware** — psychiatry, cardiology, pediatrics, emergency, OB-GYN, dermatology, ENT, orthopedics, ophthalmology, surgery and general practice.
- **Medical coding support** — ICD-10, SNOMED CT and LOINC candidates with rationale and confidence.
- **Drug formulary & interaction awareness**, with cost-saving substitutions.
- **Learns per doctor** — clinician feedback calibrates the system over time.

*OHM is positioned as a clinician-facing advisory tool. Every output is a suggestion the doctor can accept, edit or reject — the doctor issues every prescription and signs every note.*

## Section 6 — Built for hospitals, run on hospital infrastructure

**Eyebrow:** ARCHITECTURE & SOVEREIGNTY

### Your patient data never leaves your hospital.

OHM separates the clinical workspace (which runs inside the hospital) from the AI engine (which holds the intelligence). The design guarantees that **no patient-identifying data is ever stored outside the hospital's control**.

- **On-premise or sovereign cloud** — the hospital orchestrator, patient data and audit logs all live on your infrastructure.
- **Zero PHI leaves your walls** — the AI layer sees only what it needs to process a request, and stores no transcripts or patient identifiers.
- **Full audit trail** — every action logged with actor, time and change.
- **Standards-based** — HL7 FHIR R4, ABDM alignment, NHA terminology, DPDP Act 2023 compliance.
- **Interoperable** — standard APIs over your existing EMR/HMIS. No rip-and-replace.
