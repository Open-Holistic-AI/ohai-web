## Section 3 — Data sovereignty

**Eyebrow:** SOVEREIGNTY BY DESIGN

### Your data never leaves your control.

Sovereignty isn't a checkbox for us — it's the architecture.

- **Runs on your infrastructure** — on-premise or sovereign cloud, government or hospital owned.
- **Separation of concerns** — the workspace that holds sensitive data (patient records, citizen data, files) lives on your side; the AI engine sees only what a single request needs and stores no identifiers.
- **Zero-retention AI layer** — no transcripts, no patient IDs, no citizen data persisted in the intelligence layer.
- **Full audit trail** — every action logged: who, what, when.
- **DPDP Act 2023 compliant** — aligned with India's data protection law by design.
