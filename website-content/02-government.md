### Nalam AI — Health

**Hospital appointments on WhatsApp — no queue, no counter, no app.**

Launched at Rajiv Gandhi Government General Hospital, Chennai, Nalam AI puts the entire outpatient journey on a phone the citizen already owns.

- **Appointments booked on WhatsApp** — no counter, no token, no queue.
- **Prescriptions sent to the patient's phone** — nothing to collect in person.
- **Lab and scan reports delivered on WhatsApp** — no second trip to the hospital.
- **e-Visit** — a consultation without travelling to the hospital.
- **Geo-fencing** — each citizen mapped to their nearest government facility.
- **e-Seva centres assist anyone** without a smartphone.

**Result:** 1.5 M+ messages in the first month · Live across 22 districts.

---

### Ambient Listening — Health (National Health Mission, TN)

**Doctors treat. The record writes itself.**

Running at Rajiv Gandhi Government General Hospital, Chennai. The doctor speaks naturally; the system writes the clinical record — accurately, even in crowded outpatient wards through background noise and cross-talk.

- **No typing during the consultation** — the doctor's full attention stays on the patient.
- **Accurate in real ward conditions** — noise, cross-talk, code-switching between Tamil and English.
- **The record is complete before the patient leaves the room.**
- **In partnership with the National Health Mission, Tamil Nadu.**

**Result:** 90 minutes saved per doctor, every day.
