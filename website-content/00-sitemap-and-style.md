## Positioning & messaging

**One-line positioning**
> Sovereign AI — built by us, run on your infrastructure, reaching every citizen.

**Elevator pitch (50 words)**
> Open Holistic AI Solutions is a Chennai-based sovereign-AI company, backed by IIT Madras and Intel. We build and operate India's own AI models — then deploy them end-to-end inside governments, hospitals and education institutes. Not software you manage. An outcome we run for you.

**What we are (the FDE model)**
We are Forward-Deployed Engineers for the public sector. We don't hand over a licence and leave. We build the models, host them on your infrastructure, integrate with the systems you already run, and stay on to operate and support the outcome. Departments receive a working service, not a project to manage.

**Three pillars**
1. **Our own models** — post-trained language and vision-language models built in-house. The core IP is ours, not resold.
2. **Sovereign by design** — everything runs on government/hospital infrastructure. Data never leaves your control. DPDP Act 2023 compliant.
3. **Proven at scale, today** — already live inside Government of Tamil Nadu departments across health, administration and education.

## Voice & tone

- **Confident, plain, specific.** Lead with real numbers (1.5M+ messages, 90 minutes saved, 140,000 users). Avoid hype adjectives.
- **Citizen-first, outcome-first.** Frame every capability as what it does for a patient, a doctor, a student, an officer — not as a feature.
- **Sovereign and Indian.** Language-first (Tamil and English), built in India, run in India.
- **Credible.** Name the institutions, the ministers, the hospitals, the press. Proof over promises.

## Visual direction (for design team)

- **Palette:** Deep navy (`#12343B` / `#0F3A44` range) as primary, teal accent (`#1E6E7E`), clean white space, light slate for cards (`#EAF1F3`).
- **Type:** A serif display for headlines (authoritative, institutional), a clean sans for body (modern, legible). Mirrors the government proposal deck.
- **Imagery:** Real deployment photos — hospitals, launches, ministers, IIT Madras. Avoid stock AI clip-art.
- **Trust bar everywhere:** IIT Madras · Intel · IITM Pravartak logos; ABDM / NHA / DPDP compliance where relevant.
