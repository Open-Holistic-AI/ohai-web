## Section 2 — Mission

### Sovereign AI, in the service of every citizen.

We believe the most important place for AI isn't a consumer app — it's public service. A patient who no longer stands in a queue. A doctor who treats instead of types. A student with a teacher available at any hour. An officer whose files move without friction.

Our mission is to build India's own AI and run it, at scale, on infrastructure the nation controls — so the benefits reach every citizen and the data stays sovereign.

## Section 3 — What makes us different

### We're a forward-deployed engineering company, not a licence vendor.

Anyone can sell an AI subscription. We do the harder, more valuable thing: we build the models, deploy them on your infrastructure, integrate with the systems you already run, and stay on to operate and support the outcome.

- **We own our IP.** The models are ours, built in-house — not resold foreign APIs.
- **We run what we build.** You get an outcome, not a project to staff.
- **We're sovereign by design.** Your data stays on your infrastructure, under your control.
- **We're already proven.** Live inside Government of Tamil Nadu, at state scale, today.
