## Section 2 — The idea

### Give your whole organisation governed AI — not a scatter of subscriptions.

Most organisations end up with AI everywhere and control nowhere: personal accounts, unmanaged keys, no visibility. OHAI is the opposite — one identity, one set of managed models, one admin console, and clear per-user usage across every surface.

## Section 3 — Three surfaces, one account

### Web

A fast, modern workspace for chatting with AI and getting real work done.

- **Multi-model chat** — switch models on the fly, with streaming, branching, retries and full history.
- **Live artifacts** — interactive HTML, code and visuals rendered right in the conversation.
- **Documents in and out** — view PDFs, Word, Excel and CSVs inline; generate downloadable Excel, Word, PDF and PowerPoint.
- **Diagrams & charts** — flowcharts, sequence diagrams and data charts straight from a prompt.
- **Files & knowledge** — upload documents, extract their contents, bring your own reference material into a conversation.
- **Tools & integrations** — connect external tools so the assistant can act, search and work with live data.
- **Math & rich formatting**, sharing via link, and a multilingual, accessible interface in light and dark themes.

### Command line (CLI)

An AI coding agent that lives in the terminal and works directly in your projects.

- **Agentic coding** — describe a task; the agent reads, edits and creates files, runs commands and iterates to completion.
- **Built-in tools** — file read/write/edit, search, shell and web fetch, with permission controls you stay in charge of.
- **Plan and build modes** — read-only exploration, or full-access changes.
- **Any model**, **editor integration**, **browser sign-in** (no keys to copy), and **usage visibility** per project and model.
- **Automation-ready** — scriptable, usable in CI and other workflows.

### Desktop

The full experience as a native app for macOS, Windows and Linux.

- **Native app** — dedicated window, system integration, notifications and deep links.
- **The complete agent and chat experience**, packaged for the desktop and offline-friendly.
- **Auto-updates**, **multi-window & tabs**, **fast local performance**, and **one account everywhere**.
